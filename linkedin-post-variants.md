## Usage Tips:

**Best posting times (EET):**
- Monday-Friday: 8:00-9:00, 12:00-13:00, 17:00-18:00
- Avoid weekends for B2B content

**Engagement strategy:**
1. Post main variant (Variant 1)
2. Reply to comments within 1 hour
3. Ask questions to drive engagement
4. Share to relevant groups
5. Tag connections who care about i18n/localization

**Hashtag strategy:**
- 3-5 broad hashtags (#EventTech #StartupLife)
- 2-3 technical hashtags (#React #i18n)
- 1-2 location hashtags (#Estonia #BalticTech)

**Follow-up content ideas:**
- Behind-the-scenes development video
- User testimonials in different languages
- Translation accuracy comparison
- Tech deep-dive blog post
- Lessons learned article
