## Technical Direction Notes

### Voice Casting Recommendations

**Sarah (Scene 1):** Professional female voice, 30-35 vocal age, medium-high pitch, confident and warm with excellent articulation. British or Neutral American accent. Think: corporate presentation meets TED Talk energy.

**Alex (Scene 2):** Gender-neutral or young professional voice, 25-30 vocal age, conversational and friendly. North American accent preferred. Think: your friend showing you a cool app over coffee.

**Sophie (Scene 3):** Mature female voice, 40-45 vocal age, authoritative with warmth. Slight European accent (German or Nordic) adds credibility. Think: experienced professional who knows what she's talking about.

**Marcus (Scene 4):** Energetic male voice, 28-33 vocal age, fast-paced entrepreneur energy. American accent (possibly West Coast tech). Think: startup founder demo energy with genuine enthusiasm.

**Jasmine (Scene 5):** Relaxed female voice, 25-28 vocal age, warm and casual. Any accent works. Think: evening wind-down, talking to yourself while browsing, authentic and unpolished.

**Elena (Scene 6):** Executive female voice, 40-50 vocal age, commanding with sophistication. Mediterranean European accent (Italian, Spanish, or French) adds prestige. Think: boardroom presentation, C-level authority.

**David (Scene 7):** Deep male voice, 32-38 vocal age, thoughtful and visionary. Neutral accent. Think: late-night philosophical conversation, Steve Jobs presentation energy, restrained passion.

**Maya (Scene 8):** Expressive female voice, 25-30 vocal age, authentic and unpolished in best way. Local accent (could be regional American, Irish, or Australian) adds grassroots authenticity. Think: passionate community activist, genuine storyteller.

### Music Direction

Each scene requires distinct musical atmosphere:

**Scene 1:** Modern tech-forward synth music, uplifting and professional  
**Scene 2:** Acoustic indie with guitar, comfortable coffee shop vibes  
**Scene 3:** Minimalist electronic, precision and professionalism  
**Scene 4:** Upbeat indie-electronic, startup energy and innovation  
**Scene 5:** Chill electronic ambient, evening relaxation  
**Scene 6:** Orchestral corporate, strings and piano for prestige  
**Scene 7:** Ambient cinematic, Hans Zimmer-style contemplation  
**Scene 8:** Indie folk acoustic, grassroots community warmth  

### Ambiance/Foley Requirements

- Office sounds: computer fans, keyboards, distant phones, air conditioning
- Coffee shop: espresso machines, cup clinks, muffled conversations
- Conference room: HVAC, distant city sounds, paper rustle, professional space
- Apartment: distant traffic, TV from another room, cat purrs, fabric rustles
- Community center: room reverb, setup sounds, chairs moving, people talking
- Night office: silent city far below, minimal tech hums, contemplative quiet

### TTS Implementation Tags

For advanced TTS systems supporting SSML or similar markup:

- Use `<prosody rate="X%">` to adjust pace (Alex and Marcus faster, David and Elena slower)
- Use `<emphasis level="strong">` on key phrases and numbers
- Use `<break time="Xms">` for dramatic pauses (especially David's scene)
- Use `<voice emotion="excited/calm/authoritative">` if supported
- Layer ambient sounds at -18dB to -12dB relative to voice
- Music typically -24dB to -18dB, swelling to -15dB at emotional peaks
